# Terms and conditions clause library

These clauses are drafting guidance, not legal advice; have a licensed attorney review before signing. Jurisdictions differ, and a clause that protects one practice can expose another. Assemble only the clauses that fit the engagement, adapt the bracketed values, and keep this caution with any assembled terms until counsel has reviewed them.

Each clause is written in plain English at proposal length. The bracketed slots are the decisions the parties must make.

## Independent contractor

The consultant is an independent contractor. Nothing in this agreement creates employment, agency, or partnership. The consultant may serve other clients, subject to the confidentiality clause.

## Intellectual property

The consultant retains all intellectual property: methods, frameworks, tools, code, and know-how, whether pre-existing or developed under this agreement. The client owns the work product delivered to it — documents, reports, and deliverables created specifically for the client, and anything a statement of work designates as client-owned — with a perpetual, non-exclusive license to any consultant IP embedded in that work product.

## AI services

The client authorizes the consultant to process client data through third-party AI providers under those providers' terms. The consultant will use options that exclude client data from model training and will not use client confidential data to serve other customers. The client is responsible for human verification of AI output before relying on it.

## Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential. This obligation survives termination.

## Non-solicitation

For the term and [12] months after, neither party will solicit for employment the other's employees or contractors it worked with under this agreement. General job postings are not solicitation.

## Warranty and liability

Services are performed with professional skill and care; no other warranty is given. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages. The consultant's total liability is capped at fees paid in the [twelve] months preceding the claim.

## Payment

Invoices are due net [15]. Amounts more than [30] days past due accrue [1%] per month, and the consultant may pause work on them.

## Termination

Either party may terminate on [30] days' written notice. Fees earned and expenses incurred through the effective date are due. The client keeps its license to consultant IP embedded in work product paid for in full.

## Entire agreement

This document, once signed by both parties, is the entire agreement and supersedes all prior discussions. Purchase orders and vendor-portal terms do not modify it. Changes bind only in a writing signed by both parties.

## Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of [jurisdiction]. Disputes go to the courts of [venue]; the prevailing party recovers reasonable attorneys' fees.
