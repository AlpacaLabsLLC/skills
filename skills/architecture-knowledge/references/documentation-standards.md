# Documentation standards orientation

## AK-MASTERFORMAT

- **Canonical term:** MasterFormat
- **Aliases:** CSI MasterFormat
- **Authority class:** first-party
- **Boundaries:** MasterFormat is licensed context; this corpus contains no number-title list and does not declare the project-applicable edition.
- **Source IDs:** SRC-CSI-STANDARDS-OVERVIEW, SRC-CSI-MASTERFORMAT-FAQ
- `A1` | MasterFormat is a CSI classification system used to organize construction information. | Sources: `SRC-CSI-STANDARDS-OVERVIEW`
- `A2` | CSI presents MasterFormat 2026 as current at this corpus review date of 2026-08-20; a project's licensed edition remains controlling. | Sources: `SRC-CSI-STANDARDS-OVERVIEW`
- `A3` | This corpus does not reproduce MasterFormat numbers, titles, or tables. | Sources: `SRC-CSI-MASTERFORMAT-FAQ`
- `AK-MASTERFORMAT` — related-to → `AK-SECTIONFORMAT` | Sources: `SRC-CSI-STANDARDS-OVERVIEW`
