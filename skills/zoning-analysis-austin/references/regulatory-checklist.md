# Austin regulatory checklist

Mark each item Verified, Reported, Not applicable, or Not verified before calculating capacity.

## Authority and property

- Full-purpose, limited-purpose, ETJ, or other jurisdiction
- Parcel/geographic ID, legal lot context, area, and geometry
- Recorded subdivision, plat notes, easements, and development agreements checked
- Existing building footprint and impervious-cover data date

## Zoning stack

- Every base zoning polygon intersecting the parcel
- Combining districts and conditional overlays
- Zoning ordinance number, exhibits, conditions, and amendments
- Conditional-overlay tract or exhibit matched to the parcel; prohibited and conditional uses transcribed
- PUD, NCCD, TOD, ERC, regulating plan, or other special district
- Neighborhood plan and future land use kept separate from current zoning
- Approved site plan, variance, or vested-rights claim checked where material

## Development controls

- Allowed, conditional, and prohibited uses
- Minimum lot area and dimensions
- Front, side-street, interior-side, and rear setbacks
- Height and measurement method
- Floor-to-area ratio, when applicable
- Building coverage and impervious cover
- Compatibility or transition standards
- Parking, bicycle parking, loading, access, and driveway controls
- Landscape, screening, open-space, and tree requirements
- Density, unit, preservation, affordability, or mixed-use incentives

## Environmental and infrastructure

- FEMA and City fully developed floodplains
- Watershed classification and watershed regulation area
- Creek buffers and water-quality setbacks
- Edwards Aquifer recharge, contributing, and verification zones
- Erosion hazard, steep slope, critical environmental feature, and wildfire layers
- Heritage/protected trees and available canopy information
- Utilities, fire access, drainage, and right-of-way constraints identified as later design checks
- Positive envelope-screen intersections confirmed against parcel geometry; negative results phrased as no mapped intersection returned

## Quality controls

- Numeric controls cite current official provisions
- Calculations show formulas, inputs, and units
- Split zoning is not averaged without authority
- Missing values remain null or Not verified, never zero
- Code maximums are not described as guaranteed yield
- GIS-area calculations are labeled screening upper bounds rather than achievable capacity
- Report ends with the canonical professional disclaimer and marker
